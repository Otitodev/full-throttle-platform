How it
works

Three steps from zero to full automation. No engineering team required.

01

Create a Profile

One command spins up an isolated Hermes agent profile for your client — complete with its own memory, tools, and credentials. Think of it as a dedicated AI employee per account.

02

Wire the Data

Connect the profile to the client's website, social accounts, and data sources. The agent learns their industry, tone, and positioning — automatically.

03

Hit the Gas

Set your playbooks — outreach sequences, content calendars, monitoring jobs — and let the agent execute. Dashboard gives you oversight; automation does the work.
